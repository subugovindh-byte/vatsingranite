## 1. Central Government Regulations (Mining)

### 1.1 Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957
**Latest Amendment:** MMDR Amendment Act 2023

**Key Provisions:**
- **Section 8A**: State Government powers for minor minerals
- **Section 9B**: District Mineral Foundation (DMF) - 30% of royalty for major minerals, 10-30% for minor minerals (state discretion)
- **Section 9C**: National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) - 2% of royalty
- **Section 10**: Royalty rates - notified by State Government for minor minerals
- **Section 10A**: Auction-based allocation for new leases
- **Section 10B**: Premium in addition to royalty (composite licence holders)
- **Section 21**: Power to make rules for mineral conservation

**2023 Amendment Updates:**
- Captive mines allowed for sale up to 50% production in open market
- Enhanced compensation for land acquisition
- Provision for transfer of mining leases through auction
- Technology-driven enforcement mechanisms

**System Impact:** Module M15 must track DMF/NMET at current rates, dead rent vs royalty comparison, and support auction-based premium calculations for any new leases.

---

### 1.2 Mineral Conservation and Development Rules (MCDR), 2017
**Administered by:** Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM)

**Key Forms & Returns:**
- **Form A**: Application for approval of mining plan
- **Form B**: Application for approval of mine closure plan
- **Form C**: Application for modification of approved mining plan
- **Form F1**: Monthly return (mineral production, despatch, stock)
- **Form H1**: Annual return (reserves, production, machinery, personnel)
- **Form F2**: Annual statistics
- **Part A/B/C/D**: Mining plan components

**Star Rating Framework (2022-ongoing):**
- Sustainability assessment: 5-star (best) to 1-star
- Parameters: environmental, social, economic, compliance, safety
- Mandatory for mines > 5 Ha lease area (may apply to aggregated holdings)
- Public disclosure on IBM portal

**Recent Updates (2024-2026):**
- E-permit integration for inter-state mineral transport
- Quarterly DGPS survey mandatory (earlier annual for small mines)
- Mine closure fund escrow account mandatory (earlier only for major minerals)
- Progressive mine closure with annual compliance certificate

**System Impact:** Module M15 must support monthly Form F1, annual Form H1/F2 generation, quarterly DGPS reconciliation (M23), star-rating self-assessment (M29), and mine closure provision tracking (M11).

---

### 1.3 Mines Act, 1952 & Mines Rules, 1955
**Administered by:** Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS)

**Applicability:** All mines employing > 20 persons (opencast granite quarries included)

**Key Provisions:**

**Personnel & Qualifications:**
- **Mines Manager**: Statutory appointment, First/Second Class Manager's Certificate of Competency
- **Mining Sirdar/Overman**: Where > 50 persons employed
- **Blaster**: DGMS-issued Blaster's Certificate
- **First-Aider**: Certificate from St. John Ambulance or Red Cross

**Working Hours & Welfare:**
- Maximum 8-hour shift, 48-hour week
- Overtime: double rate, with DGMS permission
- Weekly rest: 1 day off in 7 days
- Paid leave: 1 day per 15 days worked (after 6 months)
- Crèche: If 50+ women employed

**Safety & Inspection:**
- Daily inspection by competent person (Rule 28)
- Bench height: ≤ 6m for manual working, ≤ 10m for mechanical (state rules vary)
- Berm width: Minimum 5m or half bench height, whichever is more
- Slope angle: As per approved mining plan (typically 60-70° for granite)
- Blasting: Minimum 30 minutes warning; area clearance; flagman; misfires handled per Rule 93

**Statutory Registers:**
- Form A: Employment register
- Form B: Persons employed
- Form C: Leave with wages
- Form D: Compensation for injury
- Form E: Accident report (fatal/serious: immediate notice to DGMS)
- Daily Report of Mines (Metalliferous Mines Regulations, 1961 Reg 9)

**2024-2026 Updates:**
- Digital registers accepted (earlier only manual)
- Electronic reporting of accidents (portal: dgms.gov.in)
- Mandatory GPS-based tracking for explosives vehicles
- Enhanced training requirements for heavy equipment operators

**System Impact:** Modules M14 (payroll with leave tracking), M15 (all statutory registers), M18 (safety, inspection, accidents), M2 (blaster certificates, blast register), M27 (training records).

---

### 1.4 Explosives Act, 1884 & Explosives Rules, 2008
**Administered by:** Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO)

**Magazine Licence:** Form IV under Rule 36
- Validity: 3 years
- Renewal: 3 months before expiry
- Maximum storage: As per licence (typically 500-2000 kg for quarry)
- Security: Watchman 24×7, boundary wall, earthen bund

**Possession Licence:** Form V (if quarry stores > 50 kg)

**Transport:** Form VI (consignment note), vehicle with flashing red light, trained driver

**Daily Magazine Register:** Rule 126
- Opening stock, receipt, issue, return, closing stock
- Batch/lot number, manufacturer, date of manufacture/expiry
- Authorised signatory

**Blaster Competency:** PESO-issued (earlier DGMS-issued grandfathered)

**2024-2026 Updates:**
- E-application for licence renewal (PESO portal)
- Digitization of magazine register accepted
- GPS tracking for explosives vehicles (pilot in several states)
- Enhanced security: biometric access, CCTV (for storage > 1000 kg)

**System Impact:** Module M2 (magazine register, licence alerts, blaster certificates), M19 (licence renewal calendar).

### 1.5 Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 & EIA Notification, 2006 (as amended 2023)

**Environmental Clearance (EC):**
- **Category B2** (minor minerals, lease < 5 Ha): State-level (SEIAA)
- **Validity:** Typically tied to mining plan period (5-10 years)
- **Conditions:** Plantation, dust suppression, water management, noise limits, reclamation
- **Compliance Reports:** Half-yearly to SEIAA/MoEF

**Consent to Establish (CTE) / Consent to Operate (CTO):**
- **Authority:** Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB)
- **Water Act, 1974** (if groundwater extraction > 10 KLD)
- **Air Act, 1981** (stone crushing, screening)
- **Validity:** 5 years (CTO), renewable
- **Conditions:** Stack monitoring, ambient air quality, noise, effluent standards

**Green Belt:** Minimum 7.5m around lease boundary or 33% of lease area, whichever is less

**2024-2026 Updates:**
- Online application and submission (parivesh.nic.in)
- Quarterly compliance via online portal (earlier half-yearly)
- Drone-based monitoring for mine closure and afforestation
- Carbon credit linkage proposed (not yet mandatory)

**System Impact:** Module M17 (EC validity tracking, CTO renewal, compliance reports, air/water/noise readings), M29 (green belt register, carbon footprint).

## 2. Tamil Nadu State Regulations (Minor Minerals)

### 2.1 Tamil Nadu Minor Mineral Concession Rules, 1959 (TNMMCR)
**Latest Amendment:** 2022 (notified 2023)

**Quarry Lease:**
- **Maximum extent:** 10 Ha (earlier 5 Ha, increased in 2019)
- **Lease period:** 5-20 years, renewable
- **Royalty:** As per Tamil Nadu Gazette notification (varies by mineral colour/grade)
  - Ordinary granite (black/grey): ₹75-100 per m³
  - Multi-colour / commercial granite: ₹120-180 per m³
  - Premium colours (Paradiso, Kashmir White, etc.): ₹200-300 per m³
- **DMF:** 10% of royalty (MMDR Act Sec 9B)
- **NMET:** 2% of royalty (MMDR Act Sec 9C)
- **Surface rent:** ₹5,000-15,000 per Ha per annum

**Lease Grant Process:**
- Application to District Collector / Assistant Director of Geology & Mining
- Field inspection and site verification
- No-objection from Forest, Revenue, Panchayat
- Gazette notification
- Execution of lease deed
- Payment of first-year rent, royalty security deposit

**Annual Permitted Quantity (APQ):**
- Specified in lease deed
- Based on approved mining plan
- Excess extraction: Penalty @ 200% of normal royalty

**Mineral Despatch Permit (MDP):**
- Mandatory for every despatch from lease
- Issued by Mining Department (online portal: https://tngranite.tn.gov.in or successor)
- Format: e-Permit with QR code
- Validity: 7 days from issue
- Linked to royalty payment and APQ balance

**Monthly Return:**
- To Assistant Director, Geology & Mining
- Format: Opening stock, production, despatch, closing stock (m³)
- Due date: 5th of following month
- Penalty for non-filing: ₹1,000 per month of delay

**2022-2023 Updates:**
- Online lease application (portal-based)
- E-permit mandatory (earlier manual challan)
- GPS-based geo-fencing for lease boundaries
- Aadhaar-linked lease holder verification
- Mobile app for field officers (inspections logged digitally)

**System Impact:** Module M15 (royalty at TN rates, DMF/NMET, APQ tracking), M19 (e-permit generation and reconciliation with TN portal), M1 (geo-fence validation against lease polygon).

### 2.2 Tamil Nadu State-Specific Bench & Safety Standards

**Tamil Nadu Mines Safety Rules (adopted from Central + State overlay):**
- **Ultimate pit depth:** ≤ 50m without special permission (typical granite quarries 20-40m)
- **Bench height:** ≤ 10m (earlier 6m; relaxed with mechanization approval)
- **Bench width:** Minimum 5m (preferably 7.5m for equipment movement)
- **Slope angle:** 60-70° (as per approved mining plan; flatter for weathered rock)
- **Safety berm:** Minimum 1.5m high earthen berm at bench edges
- **Haul road gradient:** ≤ 1 in 10 (10%)
- **Haul road width:** Minimum 1.5× widest vehicle (typically 7-10m)
- **Barrier at pit edge:** Minimum 1.5m from ultimate pit edge, no approach within 3m without barricade
- **Lighting:** If night shift operated (requires DGMS permission)
- **Water accumulation:** Dewatering mandatory, standing water ≤ 1m depth

**Blasting Standards:**
- **Proximity restrictions:**
  - Public road: Minimum 200m (or as per mining plan)
  - Habitation: 500m (or as per EC condition)
  - Railway line: 1000m
- **Blast timing:** Daylight hours only (except with written permission)
- **Warning:** 30 minutes prior, siren/horn, red flag display, area clearance
- **Vibration limit:** Peak Particle Velocity (PPV) < 5 mm/s at nearest structure (DGMS standard)
- **Flyrock control:** Blast mat, stemming, proper delay sequence
- **Misfires:** 24-hour wait, qualified blaster disposal, entry in register

**System Impact:** Module M1 (bench parameters, haul road tracking), M2 (blast proximity check, vibration log), M18 (safety inspection checklist with TN-specific parameters), M23 (3D quarry model with bench/berm/barrier validation).

### 2.3 Tamil Nadu GST & State Tax Provisions

**Tamil Nadu Goods & Services Tax Act, 2017:**
- **HSN 2516** (granite blocks/slabs): 5% GST (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) for rough/dressed blocks; 18% for polished slabs
- **HSN 6802** (worked monumental stone): 18%
- **Composition Scheme:** Not available for quarries (ineligible category)
- **E-way bill threshold (intra-TN):** ₹50,000 (Central rule; may be higher, monitor notification)
- **E-invoice threshold:** ₹5 crore aggregate turnover (Central rule; 2026 status)

**Tamil Nadu Professional Tax:**
- Slabs: ₹0 (income < ₹21,000 p.m.), ₹200-₹2,500 p.m. (higher slabs)
- Employer: Deduct and remit monthly/half-yearly to local body (Municipality / Town Panchayat / Corporation)
- Return: Half-yearly (April-Sep, Oct-Mar)

**Tamil Nadu Value Added Tax (VAT) - Legacy:**
- Abolished post-GST (2017), but pending assessments/refunds may continue
- System to retain historical VAT data for audit

**System Impact:** Module M12 (HSN 2516 @5%, HSN 6802 @18%, PT deduction per TN slabs), M10 (e-way bill with ₹50k threshold, e-invoice if applicable).

## 3. Labour & Payroll Regulations (Tamil Nadu Context)

### 3.1 Code on Wages, 2019 (Central Act, enforced 2023)
Consolidates: Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Wages Act, Payment of Bonus Act, Equal Remuneration Act

**Minimum Wages (Tamil Nadu Schedule, 2025-2026):**
- **Unskilled (quarry worker):** ₹475-520 per day (district variation; Krishnagiri ≈ ₹495)
- **Semi-skilled (driller, mason):** ₹520-580 per day
- **Skilled (blaster, operator):** ₹600-700 per day
- **Highly skilled (mines manager, surveyor):** ₹750-950 per day
- **DA:** Linked to Consumer Price Index (CPI-IW), revised half-yearly

**Payment of Wages:**
- Wages to be paid before 7th of following month (< 1000 employees)
- Mode: Bank transfer (cash only with written consent)
- Deductions: Only authorized (PF, ESI, PT, loans with limit)

**Working Hours:**
- 8 hours per day, 48 hours per week (aligned with Mines Act)
- Overtime: 2× rate for hours beyond 9 per day

**System Impact:** Module M14 (minimum wage validation per TN schedule, DA auto-update, overtime at 2×, payment by 7th).

---

### 3.2 Employee Provident Fund (EPF) & Employee State Insurance (ESI)

**EPF (Central Act, universally applicable):**
- Applicable: Establishment with ≥ 20 employees (quarries covered)
- Employee contribution: 12% of basic + DA (capped at ₹15,000 basic for statutory, voluntary higher)
- Employer contribution: 12% (8.33% to EPS, 3.67% to EPF)
- Admin charge: 0.5% + EDLI 0.5%
- Return: Monthly ECR via EPFO portal (15th of following month)
- **2024-2026 Update:** Higher pension option (EPS amendment under litigation, interim compliance required)

**ESI (Central Act):**
- Applicable: Establishment with ≥ 10 employees (quarries covered in most states including TN)
- Wage limit: ≤ ₹21,000 per month
- Employee contribution: 0.75%
- Employer contribution: 3.25%
- Return: Monthly (ESIC portal, 15th of following month)
- Benefits: Medical, sickness, maternity, disablement, dependent benefit

**System Impact:** Module M14 (auto EPF/ESI calculation, ECR file generation), M16 (monthly returns, challan generation).

---

### 3.3 Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970

**Applicability:** Principal employer engaging contract workers via contractor

**Principal Employer Registration:** Form I (validity: perpetual, but renewal if change)

**Contractor Licence:** Form V (validity: 1 year, renewable)

**Compliance:**
- Contractor to provide PF/ESI coverage to contract workers
- Principal employer jointly liable if contractor defaults
- Wage parity: Contract workers' wages ≥ minimum wages
- Half-yearly Return: Form XXIV (to Labour Commissioner)

**Tamil Nadu Specific:**
- Registration with District Labour Officer
- Inspection powers: Joint inspection of principal + contractor sites
- Penalty: ₹20,000-₹50,000 + imprisonment for violation

**System Impact:** Module M16 (contractor master with licence tracking, contract worker PF/ESI status, Form XXIV generation), M14 (bar entry of uncovered contract workers per M26).

---

### 3.4 Tamil Nadu Shops & Establishments Act, 1947 (if office/admin staff)

- Registration of establishment within 30 days of commencement
- Weekly off, leave, working hours for non-mining office staff
- Display of registration certificate

**System Impact:** Module M16 (registration certificate expiry alert), M14 (leave rules for office staff distinct from mining staff).

## 4. Taxation (Income Tax, TDS, TCS)

### 4.1 Income-tax Act, 1961 (as amended up to Finance Act 2026)

**Corporate Tax Rate (FY 2025-26, AY 2026-27):**
- Default regime: 30% + 10% surcharge (if income > ₹1 cr) + 4% cess = ≈33.38%
- Concessional regime (Sec 115BAA): 22% + 10% surcharge + 4% cess = ≈24.42% (if certain conditions met, no deductions/exemptions)
- MAT: 15% + 10% surcharge + 4% cess on book profit (if tax < MAT)

**TDS Provisions (relevant for quarries):**
- **194C** (Contractor payments): 1% (individual/HUF), 2% (company) if payment > ₹30,000 per transaction or ₹1 lakh in FY
- **194J** (Professional fees, CA/lawyer/consultant): 10%
- **194I** (Rent, machinery/land): 10%
- **194H** (Commission): 5%
- **194M** (Contract/professional to resident individual/HUF, if payer is individual/HUF not subject to tax audit): 5%
- **194Q** (Purchase of goods > ₹50 lakh in FY): 0.1% (if seller has PAN)
- **194A** (Interest, if paid to non-bank): 10%

**TCS Provision:**
- **206C(1H)** (Sale of goods): 0.1% TCS if total receipt from buyer > ₹50 lakh in FY (effective from 1 Oct 2020, continues)

**Advance Tax:** 15% by Jun 15, 45% by Sep 15, 75% by Dec 15, 100% by Mar 15

**Returns:**
- **ITR-6** (Company): Due 31 Oct (if no audit), 30 Nov (if audit)
- **Tax Audit (44AB)**: If turnover > ₹1 cr (earlier ₹10 cr for some, but mining typically ₹1 cr)

**Depreciation (Section 32, as per Income-tax Rules):**
- Building: 10% (SLM)
- Plant & machinery (quarry): 15% (WDV) - excavators, drills, compressors, wire saws
- Computers: 40%
- Vehicles (trucks/dumpers): 15% (WDV)
- Intangible (software): 25%

**2024-2026 Updates:**
- TDS rate changes (194Q threshold increase proposed in Budget 2026, monitor)
- Presumptive taxation not available for mining
- CSR threshold: 2% of average net profit of 3 preceding FYs (if NW ≥ ₹500 cr OR turnover ≥ ₹1000 cr OR PAT ≥ ₹5 cr)

**System Impact:** Module M12 (all TDS sections, TCS 206C(1H), quarterly returns 24Q/26Q/27Q, tax audit pack per 44AB, advance tax computation, depreciation per IT Rules), M11 (MAT computation).

### 4.2 Companies Act, 2013 (for Vatsin Granite Quarry Pvt. Ltd.)

**Applicability:** Private limited company

**Annual Compliance:**
- **AOC-4** (Financial statements) - within 30 days of AGM, latest 30 Nov
- **MGT-7** (Annual return) - within 60 days of AGM
- **DIR-3 KYC** (Director KYC) - Annual, by 30 Sep
- **Form 11** (INC-22A) (Active company confirmation) - By 30 Apr (if applicable, check RoC notification)

**Audit:**
- Statutory audit mandatory
- Internal audit: If paid-up capital ≥ ₹50 cr OR turnover ≥ ₹200 cr

**Related Party Transactions (Section 188):**
- Shareholder approval for material RPT
- Disclosure in Board report and financial statements

**CSR (Section 135):**
- If NW ≥ ₹500 cr OR turnover ≥ ₹1000 cr OR PAT ≥ ₹5 cr
- Spend 2% of average net profit of 3 preceding FYs
- CSR committee, policy, annual report on CSR activities

**Cost Audit (Companies (Cost Records and Audit) Rules, 2014):**
- Applicable to mining of granite if turnover ≥ ₹50 cr and product turnover ≥ ₹25 cr (Rule 3, Annexure)
- Maintain cost records (Rule 4)
- Cost audit by Cost Accountant (Rule 5-6)
- Forms: CRA-1 (cost records), CRA-3 (cost audit report), CRA-4 (compliance report)

**System Impact:** Module M11 (AOC-4/MGT-7 data pack, related party tracking, cost records per CRA-1), M29 (CSR if threshold met).

## 5. Foreign Trade & Export Compliance (Paradiso Division)

### 5.1 Foreign Trade Policy, 2023 (FTP 2023, effective 1 Apr 2023)

**IEC (Importer-Exporter Code):**
- Mandatory for export
- Online application via DGFT portal
- Validity: Perpetual (but Aadhaar/PAN linking mandatory, annual filing of Aayaat Niryaat Form ANF-2D from FY 2024-25)

**Export Procedure:**
- Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading (or airway bill)
- Shipping bill (customs declaration, online via ICEGATE)
- Let-export order (LEO) from customs

**RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products):**
- Replaced MEIS (Merchandise Exports from India Scheme) from 1 Jan 2021
- Rate: Notified per HSN (HSN 2516 granite blocks: 0.3-0.7% of FOB, check current schedule)
- Scrip issued post-shipment (ICEGATE), transferable, usable against import duties
- Validity: 36 months from issue

**Shipping Bill & EDPMS:**
- **EDPMS** (Export Data Processing and Monitoring System) - RBI portal
- Export realisation due: 9 months from date of let-export (extendable with AD bank/RBI approval)
- FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) from AD bank
- GR waiver: For exports < USD 10,000 per shipment (or as notified)

**Sanctions & Export Control:**
- Screening against UN Consolidated List, OFAC SDN, EU sanctions, India MEA restricted entities
- Mandatory for dual-use items, certain countries (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria subject to controls)
- Granite blocks: Generally unrestricted, but end-use certificate may be required by buyer country

**2024-2026 Updates:**
- E-CoO (Electronic Certificate of Origin) mandatory via DGFT portal
- Paperless export: Digital BL, e-way bill for export cargo (pilot in major ports)
- Faceless assessment by customs (80% cases, quarry blocks typically low-risk)

**System Impact:** Module M8 (IEC master, shipping bill generation, RoDTEP scrip tracking, EDPMS reconciliation, FIRC capture, sanctions screening per Refinement R1), M13 (forex realisation within 9 months, alert for overdue).

---

### 5.2 Customs Act, 1962 & Customs Tariff Act, 1975

**Export Duty:** Nil on granite blocks (HSN 2516)

**Export through ICD/Port:**
- Chennai Port, Ennore Port, Tuticorin Port (for Tamil Nadu origin)
- Container stuffing report (surveyor certificate)
- Customs examination (risk-based, typically documentary for granite)

**LUT (Letter of Undertaking) for exports without IGST:**
- Rule 96A, CGST Rules
- Validity: Annual (FY basis, renewed 1 Apr each year)
- Online submission via GST portal
- Bond/surety not required if LUT given
- Failing to realize export proceeds within extended period: IGST + interest recovery

**System Impact:** Module M8 (LUT validity tracking, annual renewal alert on 1 Apr), M12 (zero-rated supply accounting), M13 (EDPMS / FIRC reconciliation with shipped orders).

## 6. Safety, Health & Environment (Additional Standards)

### 6.1 Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions Code, 2020
**Status:** Enacted, rules notified (implementation varies by state; TN implementation as of 2024-2025)

**Consolidates:** 13 labour laws including Factories Act, Mines Act, Dock Workers Act, etc.

**Key Provisions (when fully implemented):**
- Unified registration for all establishments
- Annual return on safety
- Inter-state migrant worker registration
- Enhanced penalty provisions
- Safety officer appointment: If ≥ 500 workers (or lower threshold for hazardous process)

**Transition:** Mines Act 1952 continues to apply until state-specific notification (as of May 2026, check TN gazette)

**System Impact:** Module M16 (monitor for implementation date, dual compliance during transition).

### 6.2 National Green Tribunal (NGT) Compliance

**Jurisdiction:** Environmental disputes

**Common Orders for Quarries:**
- Dust suppression (water sprinkling twice daily)
- Noise monitoring (< 55 dB(A) day, < 45 dB(A) night at boundary)
- No operation within 500m of habitation without clearance
- Plantation and maintenance

**System Impact:** Module M17 (dust suppression log, noise readings, distance validation from habitation using GIS in M23).

### 6.3 Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991 & E.C. Act, 1923

**Public Liability Insurance:**
- Mandatory for handling hazardous substances (explosives - covered)
- Sum insured: Based on risk assessment
- Policy copy to TNPCB and District Collector

**Employees' Compensation (formerly Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923):**
- Employer liability for work-related injury/death
- Compensation: Based on wage and extent of disablement (Schedule I-IV)
- Insurance: Group personal accident / WC policy recommended

**System Impact:** Module M13 (insurance register with public liability policy), M18 (accident register, compensation calculation per EC Act schedule).

## 7. Digital Compliance & Data Privacy

### 7.1 Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
**Status:** Enacted Aug 2023, rules under drafting (as of May 2026)

**Key Principles:**
- Consent for processing personal data
- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Right to access, correction, erasure
- Data breach notification (within notified timeframe, likely 72 hours)
- Cross-border transfer: Permitted to notified countries (list pending)

**Data Fiduciary Obligations:**
- Privacy policy, consent records
- Data inventory and mapping
- Grievance redressal officer (if notified threshold met)
- Data security safeguards

**Employee Data:**
- Aadhaar: Only with consent, store masked version
- Bank details, PAN, UAN, ESI: Legitimate purpose (statutory compliance)
- Biometric: Explicit consent

**System Impact:** Module M14 (consent capture for Aadhaar/biometric, masked display, data retention policy), M20 (data inventory, breach log, DPDP compliance module), M16 (privacy policy acknowledgement).

**Cyber Insurance:** Recommended (covers data breach, ransomware, business interruption)

## 8. Recent Case Law & Precedents (Illustrative)

### 8.1 Mining & Royalty Disputes
- **State of MP vs Mahalaxmi Fabric Mills (2022):** Royalty is not a tax; states can levy additional charges (cess, fee) on minerals beyond royalty
- **Mineral Area Development Authority vs Steel Authority of India (2023):** DMF funds to be utilized for affected areas, transparency in usage

**System Impact:** Module M15 must accommodate future state-imposed cesses/levies beyond royalty/DMF/NMET (configurable rate table).

---

### 8.2 GST Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- **Safari Retreats Pvt Ltd vs Commissioner (2023):** ITC available on capital goods used in course of business, even if output is exempt (relevant if any exempt supplies)
- **Union of India vs Bharti Airtel (2024):** E-way bill non-compliance does not deny ITC if tax paid and reflected in GSTR-2B

**System Impact:** Module M12 (ITC eligibility logic, ITC reversal under Rule 42-43, Section 17(5) blocked credits for motor vehicles unless conditions met).

## 12. System Recommendations for Regulatory Compliance

### 12.1 Configuration-Driven Compliance Engine
- All royalty rates, DMF/NMET %, GST rates, TDS/TCS sections, minimum wages, PF/ESI %, PT slabs → maintained in a **rate master table** with effective-from date
- When rate changes (government notification), system admin updates rate master; historical rates preserved
- All transactions use rate applicable on transaction date (no code change required)

### 12.2 Portal Integration Architecture
- **TN Mining Portal** (e-permit): API integration where available; RPA + manual fallback where API absent; daily sync log
- **GST Portal** (GSP/ASP): Pre-integrated connector for GSTR filing, e-invoice, e-way bill
- **EPFO/ESIC**: ECR file generation in prescribed format; upload via portal or SFTP
- **DGMS/IBM Portal**: Form upload; acknowledgement capture; manual for now, API when available
- **MCA Portal**: XBRL generation for AOC-4; digital signature integration

### 12.3 Compliance Dashboard (M21 - Enhanced)
- **Tile view:** Each statutory obligation as a tile (green = compliant, amber = due in 15 days, red = overdue)
- **Calendar view:** All due dates, clickable to form/report
- **Drill-down:** Click on tile → view form/return → file/upload → capture acknowledgement → attach evidence → auto-update tile
- **Escalation:** Auto-email to compliance officer (7 days before), HOD (3 days), MD (on due date)
- **Board pack:** One-click export of compliance status for board meeting

### 12.4 Audit Trail & Immutability
- Every financial transaction, statutory register entry, rate change, master update → immutable log (append-only table)
- Log fields: user, timestamp, IP, before-value, after-value, reason
- Audit log export for auditor with digital signature

### 12.5 Geo-Spatial Compliance (M23 - Enhanced)
- **Lease polygon:** GPS coordinates stored; display on map (Google Maps / OpenStreetMap)
- **Blast geo-tag validation:** Every blast event's lat-long checked against lease polygon; alert if outside
- **Proximity check:** Haul road, habitation, public road, railway plotted; blast proximity auto-validated
- **Drone survey import:** Point-cloud / DEM → volumetric cut-fill → reconcile with M1 production

### 12.6 Multi-Lingual & Accessibility
- **Tamil UI:** Essential for field roles (foreman, operator, watchman) - Menu, buttons, error messages
- **Voice input:** For attendance, fuel issue (speech-to-text, Tamil + English)
- **Offline mode:** Mobile app (M22) caches data; syncs when connectivity restored
- **Accessibility:** Screen reader support for visually impaired (WCAG 2.1 AA)

## 13. Regulatory Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rate change (royalty/GST) not updated in system** | Medium | High | Rate master with effective-date versioning; alert to admin on govt gazette notification |
| **E-permit portal down during despatch** | Medium | High | Offline fallback: manual permit with post-facto sync; QR pre-generated; alert to TN dept |
| **Incorrect cubic metre calculation (royalty dispute)** | Low | Very High | Triple verification: surveyor measurement, system calculation, quarterly DGPS reconciliation; photographic evidence |
| **PF/ESI non-compliance (contract workers)** | Medium | High | Bar entry at gate (M26) if contractor staff not PF/ESI covered; auto-check in system |
| **DGMS accident reporting missed (fatal/serious)** | Low | Very High | Auto SMS/email to compliance officer on accident entry; 24hr/72hr countdown timer; escalation to MD |
| **LUT not renewed (export IGST liability)** | Medium | High | Annual alert on 15 Mar (renew LUT by 1 Apr); block export order creation if LUT expired |
| **Blasting outside lease boundary** | Low | Very High | GPS geo-fence on blast event entry; auto-reject if outside polygon; mining engineer override with reason (audit trail) |
| **Data breach (employee PII / financial data)** | Low | High | Encryption, MFA, role-based access, VAPT annual, cyber insurance, breach response plan (72hr notification per DPDP) |
| **Overdue EDPMS (export realisation)** | Medium | Medium | Auto-alert at 8 months; escalation at 9 months; dashboard for export manager; AD bank reminder automation |
| **Cost audit applicability not identified** | Low | Medium | Threshold check (turnover ≥ ₹50 cr, product ≥ ₹25 cr) at year-end; auto-trigger cost records module if met |
