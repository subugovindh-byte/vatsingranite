# Vatsin Granite - Quarry Management System

## Overview
Comprehensive quarry management system for Vatsin Granite operations, compliant with Tamil Nadu and Indian Government regulations.

## Features
- License & Permit Management
- Production & Operations Management
- Sales & Commercial Management
- Financial Accounting & Compliance
- Government Reporting & Returns
- Safety & Environmental Monitoring
- Equipment & Maintenance Tracking
- HR & Payroll Management

- Software Solution Specification

## Compliance
System designed to comply with:
- Tamil Nadu Minor Mineral Concession Rules
- MMDR Act, 1957 & Amendment Act 2015
- GST Act, 2017
- Mines Act, 1952
- Environmental Protection Act, 1986
- Labour Laws (PF, ESI, Contract Labour, etc.)
